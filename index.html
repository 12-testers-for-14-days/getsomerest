FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

Everything you need to know about Google Play's 12-tester requirement and how we guarantee compliance.

How do you provide real testers?+

We maintain a vetted network of real Android users across diverse devices and geographies. Once you submit your closed testing track link, we deploy testers who download your app via physical devices — never emulators — and maintain daily engagement for exactly 14 consecutive days, meeting Google Play's production access requirements precisely.

Is this safe? Will Google detect it?+

Yes, it's completely safe. Our service uses 100% genuine Android hardware with carrier SIM cards and residential IP addresses. Usage patterns are human and natural. Google Play's integrity systems look for emulators, VPNs, and bots — our testers exhibit none of those signals. This is indistinguishable from organic tester acquisition.

What happens if a tester drops before 14 days?+

If tester count drops below 12, Google Play will reset the 14-day clock — forcing you to restart entirely. This is exactly why our Professional plan includes 4 buffer testers (16 total). If any tester becomes inactive, a buffer immediately takes their place, maintaining compliance throughout.

What devices and Android versions are covered?+

Our matrix covers Samsung Galaxy (A & S series), Xiaomi/Redmi (MIUI/HyperOS), Google Pixel, OnePlus (OxygenOS), Oppo (ColorOS), and Motorola running Android 8.0 through 16 (API 26–36). The Enterprise plan covers all eight major manufacturers with a full OS version spread — capturing manufacturer-specific quirks like MIUI battery optimization and Samsung One UI behaviors.

How fast can I get production access?+

Exactly 14 days from when testers are deployed. Google enforces this window strictly to analyze crash rates, ANRs, and retention curves. This cannot be shortened by any means. Our service guarantees all testers remain active for the full 336-hour period — so your first production submission is your only one.

Do testers provide crash reports and bug feedback?+

Yes. Beyond compliance data, testers document ANR errors, crashes, UI layout issues on specific devices, and functional bugs. This data surfaces in your Play Console pre-launch report in real time, giving you actionable insight to fix stability issues before your public release. Professional and Enterprise plans include detailed daily written reports.

What do I need to provide?+

Just your Google Play Console closed testing opt-in URL. Your app must be uploaded to the Closed Testing track with an active opt-in link. We handle tester recruitment, device provisioning, daily engagement, and compliance documentation. No SDK integration, no code changes, no technical setup required on your end.

Is this considered fake engagement or policy violation?+

No. This is managed compliance testing — not review manipulation. We do not incentivize star ratings or guarantee positive reviews. Testers provide genuine usage data and may leave organic feedback if they choose. The service is scoped entirely to meeting Google Play's technical testing requirements. No policy is violated.
